Since 1977

A Stanley Street institution.

Partner's Pub has been one of Stevens Point's favorite local taverns since 1977. That's nearly 50 years of Friday fish fries, birthday celebrations, and trivia nights at the same address on Stanley Street.

We pour New Glarus, Stevens Point Brewery, Leinenkugels, and High Life on tap. The menu runs from cheese curds to the Gut Buster. Prices are still in single digits on most starters. Everyone feels welcome here, local professionals, UWSP students, and regulars who've been coming in since the late 80s.

Come in and stay a while.
